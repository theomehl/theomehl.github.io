I was thinking about [memories](/blog/buttressed-memories/) again recently, specifically the fact that for many of the older ones, I can't tell if I actually remember a moment or if I just remember the picture I've seen of that moment. So it brings me quite a bit of joy to remember a small detail and know for certain there's no picture of it. An actual memory.
